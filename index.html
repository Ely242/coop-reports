The role

Job Description

As a Software Developer Co-op in Purolator's Digital Lab, I worked with developers, designers, and product stakeholders to build and improve internal digital tools.

My responsibilities included understanding requirements, implementing features, testing changes, investigating bugs, and contributing to code reviews. I also learned how software moves from an idea to a reliable production release.

Conclusions

The experience confirmed my interest in software development and gave me a strong foundation for future co-op terms and projects.
