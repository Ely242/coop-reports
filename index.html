Summer 2026 Co-op Report

Software Developer Co-op @ Purolator

My experience working as a software developer co-op in Purolator's Digital Lab division during Summer 2026 where I contributed to the development of internal tools and applications, collaborating to deliver real features to production.

Introduction

This Summer 2026 Co-op work term, I got the opportunity to work as a Software Developer for one of Canada's largest courier companies, Purolator. I worked in the digital lab division alongside an amazing team of innovators to build, research, and develop projects for the company.

In this website, I will be sharing my experience, the projects I worked on, and the skills I developed during this co-op term.

About Purolator Digital Lab

The Purolator Digital Lab (PDL) is the technology innovation and applied research and development department of Purolator Inc., operating as a fast-paced startup within the larger Canadian logistics company. PDL focuses on the rapid development of engineered technologies and builds systems that support Purolator's freight, parcel, and logistics operations.

The position offered a modern technology stack, mentorship from experienced engineers, and a collaborative team environment. The work was full-time hybrid, with regular hours from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., and gave me the opportunity to contribute to technology used by Canadians every day.

The role

Job Description

As a Software Developer Co-op in Purolator's Digital Lab, I worked within a specialist software engineering team to build, maintain, and improve software supporting Purolator's logistics infrastructure. The role included developing APIs, events, cloud functions, and services while resolving bugs and improving the user experience.

I collaborated with Senior Developers, Team Leads, and QA to design technical solutions, participate in code reviews and sprint planning, and become familiar with CI/CD pipelines and frequent releases. The position was a four-month, full-time hybrid role based in Kitchener, Ontario.

Technologies

  • C# and .NET
  • JavaScript, React, and TypeScript
  • Terraform and Microsoft Azure
  • SQL and NoSQL cloud-based data stores
  • Azure DevOps for work assignments, pipelines, and releases
  • Generative AI tools and agent-based development

Selected project

Internal Ideas Platform

One of my main projects was helping modernize an internal web platform that gives employees a place to share suggestions and ideas. The project focused on improving the site's appearance, usability, and consistency for a broad internal audience.

Technical Contributions

I worked on a CMS-based website theme using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and reusable templates. My work included updating page layouts, building navigation, adding animations, and creating reusable components for future development.

Skills Developed

I gained practical experience with responsive design and used design mockups to maintain consistent spacing, typography, and colour across mobile, tablet, and desktop screen sizes. I also strengthened my debugging and frontend development practices.

Team Experience

The project introduced me to everyday software development practices including code reviews, sprint planning, task tracking, version control, demos, and collaborating with a design team.

Selected project

Warehouse Automation Orchestrator

I also worked on a .NET proof-of-concept application that coordinates warehouse automation systems. The application receives telemetry and commands, maintains a canonical view of the system state, and publishes synchronized updates for other software components.

System Design

The project used an event-driven message-bus architecture to process robot and facility updates asynchronously. I gained experience designing extensible message handlers and keeping state changes consistent across distributed components.

AI-Assisted Decisions

The application included a service that used an LLM to evaluate system conditions and produce structured movement decisions. This introduced me to integrating AI capabilities with application logic, validation, and error handling.

Skills Developed

Working on the project strengthened my skills in C# and .NET, asynchronous programming, dependency injection, cloud messaging, JSON serialization, structured logging, and automated testing.

Goals / Learning Outcomes

Goal 1 - Technological Literacy

Build practical familiarity with the modern development stack used by the Digital Lab, including C#, .NET, React, TypeScript, Terraform, Azure, and cloud data stores.

Action Plan

  • Learn the team's codebase, development workflow, and Azure DevOps pipelines through hands-on tasks.
  • Use mentorship and code reviews to improve the quality and maintainability of each implementation.

Measures of Success

Success means being able to explain the purpose of the tools I use and contribute confidently to features, tests, releases, and troubleshooting across the stack.

Reflection

This work term expanded my understanding of technological literacy beyond learning individual programming languages. On the internal web platform, I applied HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and PHP while learning how a CMS theme and its templates fit together. On the automation project, I worked with C# and .NET, message-based systems, asynchronous code, cloud messaging, and LLM integration. Seeing these technologies in real projects helped me understand how frontend, backend, infrastructure, and AI-assisted tools connect to support a complete solution.

Goal 2 - Professional Development

Develop the communication and collaboration habits needed to work effectively with Senior Developers, Team Leads, QA, and other stakeholders.

Action Plan

  • Participate in code reviews, sprint planning, and team meetings.
  • Practice communicating technical questions and progress clearly in a hybrid work environment.

Measures of Success

Success means contributing constructively to discussions, responding to feedback, and keeping work organized while meeting sprint and release expectations.

Reflection

This co-op showed me that professional development is shaped just as much by collaboration as by technical ability. Through code reviews, sprint planning, demos, task tracking, and regular feedback, I became more comfortable explaining my decisions and asking questions when I needed context. I also learned to move between independent work and collaboration with developers, QA, designers, and other stakeholders. Working in a hybrid team helped me become more organized and deliberate in communicating progress, problems, and next steps.

Goal 3 - Problem Solving

Strengthen my ability to investigate bugs, understand unfamiliar systems, and design reliable solutions for real logistics problems.

Action Plan

  • Break complex requirements into smaller tasks and validate changes through testing and code review.
  • Use documentation, team feedback, and troubleshooting to resolve issues efficiently.

Measures of Success

Success means delivering changes that are efficient, carefully tested, and reliable enough to move through the team's release process.

Reflection

The projects made problem solving feel more systematic and practical. Updating a website required me to diagnose layout and responsive-design issues, while the automation project required me to think about asynchronous events, changing system state, and how separate components communicate. Debugging these kinds of problems taught me to break large issues into smaller parts, use logs and documentation, test assumptions, and ask for feedback when necessary. I am leaving the term with a stronger appreciation for careful error handling and testing, especially when building software that must behave reliably in production.

Looking back

Conclusions

This co-op gave me practical experience building software in a professional team and a clearer understanding of the habits needed to contribute effectively. I strengthened my technical skills while becoming more confident communicating, asking questions, and solving unfamiliar problems.

The experience confirmed my interest in software development and gave me a strong foundation for future co-op terms and projects.
